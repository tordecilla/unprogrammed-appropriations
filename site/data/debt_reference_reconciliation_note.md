# Annex O and P debt-reference reconciliation

Amounts in Annexes O and P are printed in million pesos. Annex O's annual interest, amortization, domestic/foreign, and grand totals were visually checked against the transcribed rows. Two instrument-level exceptions remain as printed:

- Annex O PDF p. 1: the 2014 domestic national-government instrument rows sum to 220,494, while the printed domestic NG subtotal is 220,495, a difference of one million pesos.
- Annex O PDF p. 2: the transcribed domestic NG instrument rows are zero for 1986 and 1988, while the printed domestic NG subtotals are 13,845 and 25,710, respectively. The printed table notes that breakdown totals may differ because of rounding; these larger gaps are retained as unresolved rather than attributed to rounding.

Annex P PDF p. 1 prints 2025 principal amortization as domestic 987,050, foreign 219,094, and total 1,206,145. The two components sum to 1,206,144, one million pesos below the printed total. The source values were confirmed at high zoom and retained. Annex N's 2024 principal-amortization appropriation is a separate source and is not treated as the same measure as Annex P's debt-service outturn/outlook.
