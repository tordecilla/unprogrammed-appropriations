# Annex M Table B.9 reconciliation

Amounts in the source table are in thousand pesos. Detail-row sums for 2024 and 2026 match the printed grand totals on PDF p. 19. For 2025, the detail-row total also matches the printed grand total, but its MOOE components sum to 18,000 thousand pesos more than the printed MOOE grand total.

The difference is present within a printed row on PDF p. 1: the Philippine Carabao Center's 2025 classes are PS 137,746, MOOE 553,534, and CO 182,890, summing to 874,170, while the printed row total is 856,170. The 18,000 difference equals the MOOE grand-total residual. Both printed figures are retained in the dataset with a review note.

A separate 450-thousand-peso transcription discrepancy was resolved by rechecking PDF p. 4: Batangas State University's 2025 MOOE is 1,227,276 and printed total is 2,001,544. Annex M PDF p. 20 repeats six rows and the grand total from p. 19; repeated rows are flagged and excluded from detail-sum reconciliation.
